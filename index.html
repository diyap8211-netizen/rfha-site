ABOUT US

Who We Are

We are a passionate group of students from Birla Open Minds, Kollur, Hyderabad, united by a vision to create meaningful change for women in our communities.

Through awareness campaigns, fundraising, and grassroots initiatives, we strive to uplift women by supporting education, safety, independence, and dignity.

All funds raised are directed toward trusted nonprofit organizations such as SEWA, Snehalaya, and CARE India — organizations dedicated to women's empowerment and well-being.

By turning ideas into action, we hope to build a future where every woman feels respected, supported, and empowered.
